# Deep Hearth 0.1.0 Alpha 4

Deep Hearth Alpha 4 is the **Responsive Neighborhood Alpha** from Basin Game
Studios. It is a technical reference-body update, not a Beta release.

Alpha 3 remains available as a separate historical release and a strong golden
seed for people who want to build from the earlier body.

## Responsive Neighborhood Alpha

Alpha 4 extends the golden seed into one bounded Waterdeep neighborhood:

```text
household
-> baker job
-> guild or institution
-> shared resource
-> ecological pressure
-> terrain problem
-> knowledge boundary
-> player intervention
-> persistent consequence
-> Rest
-> return
```

The purpose is not to simulate Waterdeep. It is to demonstrate that a populated
place can answer back through people, jobs, relationships, knowledge, community,
economy, ecology, terrain, care, and memory.

## System Infusion

- Generic residents can begin as job templates and deepen through first touch.
- Jobs are public community roles, not second classes or fixed permutations.
- Relationships, knowledge, privacy, community consequences, and State Capsules
  now cross individual and higher social bodies explicitly.
- The Familiar System now carries the unified physics through user intent,
  relationship attractors, hidden state, phase conflict, residue, witness,
  repair, community consequence, Rest, and departure.
- Existing campaign modules can receive a Deep Hearth conversion projection through
  job stamps, body projections, relationships, ecology, T-Stops, witnesses, and
  return state.
- Beta requirements now include full world-scale support, gods and worship, shared
  physics across planes, a robust Monster Manual, separated publication surfaces,
  d20/OGL-compatible Forgotten Realms parity, and one complete campaign module.

## Calculation and State Closure

- Outcome bands now have explicit precedence.
- Opposed transitions use canonical capacities and lead bands.
- Conditions, care, Reserve, still points, party registers, combat cycles, boar
  charge, growth Load, land slots, organ latency, spell focus, beast registers,
  and scenario clocks have clearer owners, triggers, ranges, and recovery paths.
- The Alpha 4 neighborhood has initial registers, first-touch conditions, privacy
  boundaries, propagation rules, and spirit-first transition defaults.

## Release Boundary

This remains an Alpha technical reference body. The later Beta label requires real
human playtest reports from real tables. Software simulation and automated
walkthroughs are useful supplementary evidence but cannot replace that gate.

The next deliberate work is to tighten numbers and calculation procedures through
play and then prepare the separated Player Guide, GM Guide, and Bestiary surfaces.
