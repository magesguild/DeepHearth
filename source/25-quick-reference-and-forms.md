# Quick Reference and Forms

## Action procedure

```text
declare intent
-> check deterministic physics
-> declare cost and boundary
-> choose capacity and help
-> identify uncertainty
-> roll declared die if needed
-> apply outcome
-> record state and trace
-> continue or Rest
```

## Outcome bands

```text
Demand + 5 or more -> strong success
Demand or more     -> success
Demand - 4 to - 1  -> strained result
Demand - 5 or less -> failure
```
