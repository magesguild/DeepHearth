# The Wayfinder Arrives

## A second body

The Reciprocal Trace has made room for another body. The Wayfinder arrives not
because the room summoned one, but because a player, character, or later session
chooses to attend to the invitation.

The Wayfinder is not yet a complete class. This is the smallest body that can
receive a route without pretending to own its meaning.

```text
Sense: +3
Route Thread: 1
Boundary: do not claim what the trace does not show
Rest: mark the route and return to a witnessed state
```

The Alchemist made a relation possible. The Wayfinder gives that relation
orientation.

## This minigame

```text
question: can a relation be followed without inventing its meaning?
initial state: Reciprocal Trace and unknown destination
participants: Wayfinder, existing witnesses, and possible party
moves: inspect, follow, mark, preserve, refuse, Rest
apparatus: route thread, trace, map, State Capsule
uncertainty: continuation beyond the visible far side
exit: direction, continuation, Flicker, uncertainty, or Rest
T-Stop set: CONTINUE / REFUSED / UNKNOWN / REST / RECOVERING
```

The Wayfinder's minigame computes attention and route state. It does not force
the Wayfinder to follow what it can perceive.

## Dice habitat

```text
primary die: d20 hidden route question
support dice: none
persistent registers: Route Thread and route state
entropy budget: one route question
witness: die result, route answer, and State Capsule
```

The d20 may reveal direction or continuation, but it cannot manufacture a
destination, a motive, or a desire to follow.

## The question

The Wayfinder asks:

> Can I follow a relation without pretending to know its meaning?

The visible answer is limited:

```text
origin: far side
current witness: threshold
route: reciprocal
destination: unknown
return: marked
```

The Wayfinder may add information only through a declared observation, action,
or witnessed transition.

## Receiving the trace

The Wayfinder may inspect the Reciprocal Trace without a roll. The trace is
public and already witnessed.

Record:

```text
Trace observed: yes
Direction: far side <-> threshold
Sample relation: present / absent
Destination visible: no
Return marker: present
```

The Wayfinder does not discover a destination merely by being the Wayfinder.
The class membrane creates a way to attend, not a license to invent.

## Knowledge as a shared resource

The Wayfinder's first economic contribution is not a commodity. It is a more
accurate shared map.

```text
private observation
-> declared report
-> party receiving state
-> shared route memory
-> new legal actions
```

The Alchemist may receive, question, archive, or refuse the report. The
Wayfinder may keep an observation private when its boundary requires that. No
body owes the party every thought merely because it joined the party.

## The route choices

The Wayfinder chooses one legal response:

### Follow

The Wayfinder declares a route question, such as:

> Where does this relation continue from the threshold?

If the answer is already established by the room, resolve it deterministically.
If the answer is hidden, unstable, or contested, the facilitator defines the
new uncertainty and Demand before any roll.

### Mark

The Wayfinder uses the Route Thread to create a portable route record:

```text
origin: far side
current: threshold
return: marked
unknown: destination
thread: 1 -> spent
```

The thread does not create a path. It preserves a witnessed relation so another
body can find the same question later.

### Preserve

The Wayfinder leaves the trace untouched and records it in the party Memory.
The route remains available at the threshold without becoming an obligation.

### Refuse

The Wayfinder may decline to follow, mark, or carry the relation. The invitation
remains available, and the refusal is not a failure.

## One hidden question

The first Wayfinder question with genuine uncertainty is:

> Does the reciprocal relation continue beyond the visible far side?

The facilitator must declare what makes this uncertain before asking for a roll.
For this seed, use:

```text
d20 + Sense (+3)
Demand: 12
```

Use the established outcome bands. The result changes what the Wayfinder can
legally know:

- **Strong success:** a further direction becomes visible, but not its meaning;
- **Success:** the relation continues, but the destination remains unknown;
- **Strained result:** the Wayfinder senses a continuation and marks Flicker on
  the route;
- **Failure:** no continuation can be established; the trace remains valid and
  the Wayfinder records uncertainty rather than inventing absence.

The roll answers the hidden route question. It does not decide whether the
Wayfinder wants to follow it.

## Joining the party

The Wayfinder may join the Alchemist's party, remain a separate witness, or
leave the threshold entirely.

If the Wayfinder joins, the party records:

```text
new body: Wayfinder
shared purpose: __________________
private boundary: ________________
contribution: route attention
cost: Route Thread or declared action
refusal: preserved
```

Joining does not erase the Alchemist's memory, replace the Alchemist's choice,
or make the two bodies a hive mind. It creates an interoperable relation.

## State Capsule addition

Add these fields when the Wayfinder is present:

```text
Wayfinder witness: ______________

Private boundary: _______________
```

## Completion test

This slice is complete when a group can:

- introduce a second body without a summon or command;
- inspect a public trace without a needless roll;
- distinguish route evidence from route meaning;
- follow, mark, preserve, or refuse an invitation;
- resolve one genuinely hidden route question;
- join the party without erasing individual boundaries;
- preserve the Wayfinder's observations in a State Capsule;
- Rest with the destination still allowed to be unknown.

The Wayfinder has not solved the route. It has made the unknown honest enough to
travel with.
