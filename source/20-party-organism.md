# The Party Organism

## Distinct cells, shared body

The party is a multicellular organism whose bodies remain distinct, conscious,
and consenting.

The party sheet carries:

| Register | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cohesion | ability to coordinate and exchange signals |
| Reserve | shared supplies, time, energy, and recovery |
| Integrity | capacity to remain a functioning group |
| Memory | maps, promises, discoveries, and consequences |

Use three marks for each register unless the scenario declares another scale.

## Collective action

1. One body declares the shared intent.
2. Other bodies declare contributions, boundaries, and costs.
3. The party checks that every contribution has a legal receiving state.
4. Resolve one shared transition.
5. Update individual and party state.
6. Enter the result into shared Memory.

```text
party intent
-> contributions
-> one witnessed transition
-> changed bodies and world
```

## Disagreement

Disagreement is not automatic loss of Cohesion. A body may:

- refuse;
- delay;
- propose another intent;
- preserve private memory;
- leave the formation;
- ask for Rest;
- accept a declared shared consequence.

Cohesion changes only when coordination, trust, or signal exchange actually
changes.

## Shared resources

Shared Reserve includes only resources the party has explicitly made shared.
Private inventory remains private. A character may offer a resource without
transferring custody.

The party cannot spend a member's body, consent, or private memory as a shared
resource.

Alignment differences do not automatically dissolve a party. They may change
trust, faction access, divine access, familiar relationships, or the receiving
state of a shared action. The party records those consequences rather than
forcing every member into one alignment.

The same boundary applies to organs and familiars. A party may receive a
declared organ output, but it does not automatically receive the organ, its
private transcript, its input packets, or a familiar's consent.

## Party Rest

The party may Rest when it reaches a declared safe or still point. Rest records:

```text
location
shared purpose
individual boundaries
resources spent
conditions changed
promises made
open consequences
next return point
```

Rest may restore Reserve or Integrity only through a declared care path. It does
not erase consequences merely because the scene ended.
