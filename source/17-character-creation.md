# Character Creation

## The four capacities

Every alpha character has four capacities:

| Capacity | What it carries |
| --- | --- |
| Body | force, endurance, movement, physical integrity |
| Sense | perception, route, timing, reading signals |
| Craft | tools, preparation, transformation, repair |
| Will | focus, boundaries, promises, refusal, social pressure |

Capacities are not personality labels. They describe what a body can currently
contribute to a declared transition.

## Demand

Use the smallest Demand that makes the uncertainty matter:

| Demand | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | routine but uncertain |
| 12 | standard consequential action |
| 16 | difficult or pressured action |
| 20 | extreme action with a narrow path |
| 24 | legendary action requiring strong help or special conditions |

Outcome bands remain:

```text
Demand + 5 or more -> strong success
Demand or more     -> success
Demand - 4 to - 1  -> strained result
Demand - 5 or less -> failure
```

## Creation procedure

1. Choose a species membrane.
2. Copy its four capacities.
3. Choose a name, body description, relationship, and Rest practice.
4. Take the species starting kit.
5. Write one boundary and one promise.
6. Choose one growth question.
7. Record `Integrity 3`, `Reserve 3`, and `Condition clear`.
8. Name a question the character wants to carry into the world.

9. Choose an alignment and write one anchor for each axis.

10. Record native, licensed, learned, and restricted land channels.

The player may also declare:

```text
Familiar: none / desired / present
Relationship: __________________
Familiar boundary: ______________
Alignment: ______________________
Law/Chaos anchor: _______________
Good/Evil anchor: ________________
Native land channels: ____________
Licensed channels: _______________
Learned channels: ________________
Restricted channels: _____________
Active land slots: _______________
```

No character is incomplete without a familiar. Choosing none is a complete
character state. A desired familiar is an open relationship, not an owned item
or guaranteed summon.

The character is complete when the player can answer:

```text
What do I sense?
What can I contribute?
What do I consume?
What do I protect?
What can I refuse?
How do I recover?
What does the world remember about me?
```

## Integrity and Reserve

**Integrity** is the body's capacity to remain a functioning cell under
pressure. It is not a measure of worth.

**Reserve** is immediately available material, attention, energy, time, or
permission. A class may represent Reserve differently, but spending it must
change what can happen next.

At zero Integrity, the player chooses a legal still point: withdraw,
incapacitate, surrender, shelter, or another declared recovery state. Death is a
world transition, not an automatic arithmetic result.

At zero Reserve, the character may still act through ordinary capability,
refusal, speech, movement, or Rest. They cannot silently pay a cost they no
longer possess.

## Conditions

A condition must name the action space it changes.

| Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Pressured | One declared action carries an additional pressure |
| Injured | One body field or capacity is specifically limited |
| Fatigued | The next Rest or cycle budget is reduced as declared |
| Bound | Named movement, object, or boundary actions are unavailable |
| Flicker | A route or process remains available but unstable |
| Alerted | A world cell has changed its response state |
| Uncertain | A fact is unresolved, not a penalty |
| Still | The body is safe, paused, and awaiting recovery or return |

Conditions are not generic punishment. Removing one requires a declared repair,
change of state, fulfilled condition, or Rest path.

## Growth

At a meaningful Rest, the player may answer one growth question:

```text
What did I learn?
What boundary changed?
What can I now sense?
What can I now contribute?
What must I protect differently?
```

Growth may add a word, improve a capacity, install an organ, deepen a
relationship, change a Rest practice, or create a new refusal condition. It
must leave a trace in character memory.
