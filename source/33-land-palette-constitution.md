# The Land Palette Constitution

## Channels are ecologies

Land channels are not a morality chart. They are resource ecologies with
different ways of sensing, spending, growing, protecting, transforming, and
remembering.

```text
White -> order, community, protection, obligation
Blue  -> inquiry, transformation, signal, adaptation
Black -> cost, memory, death, extraction, autonomy
Red   -> freedom, force, passion, disruption
Green -> life, growth, body, interdependence, place
```

No channel is universally stronger. Each has gifts, blind spots, costs, and
relationships it cannot enter without work.

## Access states

Every character and organ records four access states:

```text
native    -> class membrane provides efficient access
licensed  -> a faction, place, object, or relationship permits access
learned   -> sustained personal effort has integrated access
restricted -> a current alignment, host, or boundary refuses access
```

Access is not possession. A character may use a land source without owning it,
or own a card that cannot legally operate in the current habitat.

When access states conflict, use this precedence:

```text
restricted -> no operation unless a named repair or release path changes it
native     -> efficient access when the source and host accept it
licensed   -> access while the named permission remains valid
learned    -> access through sustained practice and available capacity
```

`Native`, `licensed`, and `learned` are not cumulative discounts. A source
chooses one active access state for an operation and names its cost, slot, and
refusal behavior. A character begins with two active channels and may not
silently exceed that capacity.

## Active palette

At alpha creation, a character may keep two native channels active. Additional
channels require a declared path and active capacity.

An active channel occupies one land slot. Changing an active palette requires a
declared latency, cycle cost, Rest, or machine operation. The cost is visible
before the change.

The Artificer exception is breadth, not free power:

```text
any channel available through machine conversion
no automatic native efficiency
retuning cost
machine slot cost
maintenance and failure
```

The Artificer can reach any channel but cannot run every channel at once.

## Alignment compatibility

A land source declares its alignment response:

```text
accepted alignments: _________________
refused alignments: ___________________
changed effect: _______________________
unlock path: _________________________
```

Alignment does not prevent the player from attempting access. The source may
refuse, change cost, alter effect, or record an alignment trace.

## Unlocking a channel

A character may pursue a new channel for their own reasons:

```text
declare desire
-> meet a compatible source
-> accept a cost or obligation
-> perform a witnessed act
-> act under pressure
-> Rest with the trace
-> bind the channel
```

The path may involve a faction, familiar, mentor, plane, artifact, sacrifice,
or sustained practice. The player chooses whether the desire is worth pursuing.

Unlocking a channel grants access, not mastery. New operations still require
cards, words, equipment, slots, cycles, Reserve, and receiving states.

Land access is a growth or loot result, not a free level bonus. A licensed source
may be active only while its place, faction, relationship, or alignment contract
remains satisfied.

## Loot and land

A land card or object is a capability packet:

```text
source
channel
cost
active slots
alignment compatibility
operation
latency
failure
cardless fallback
T-Stop set
```

Loot may be accepted, refused, archived, traded, licensed, repaired, or learned.
The GM does not need to give a player a channel as a reward. The world may offer
a path, and the player decides whether to enter it.

Every land source also names what happens when access ends:

```text
REFUSED / WITHDRAWN / UNAVAILABLE / RECOVERING / REST / CONTINUE
```

Closing a channel does not erase what was learned or silently sever a dependent
body. It records the boundary and the next legal route.

## GM land deck

The GM may maintain a bounded deck of land sources:

```text
terrain source
faction source
commons source
dangerous source
artifact source
planar source
```

Each draw must have a habitat, trigger, cost, visibility, and witness. The GM
deck can interact with player decks and organs, but it cannot bypass the same
channel and alignment rules it gives the world.

## Cardless channels

Deep Hearth remains playable without Magic cards. A cardless land source may be:

- a colored token;
- a marked land card made for the scenario;
- a place or altar;
- a faction license;
- a relationship;
- a declared energy tag;
- a physical material.

The cardless source must preserve the operation's input, output, cost, scope,
latency, and witness shape.

## Power budget

Land breadth is balanced against land depth:

```text
breadth -> more channels, more setup, more switching, less native efficiency
depth   -> fewer channels, cheaper operations, deeper cards, stronger affinity
```

No land channel grants free actions, unlimited cycles, unbounded loops, or
automatic success. A color combination becomes powerful through relationships,
cards, equipment, and practice, all of which remain visible and interruptible.

## Completion test

This slice is complete when a group can:

- record a character's native, licensed, learned, and restricted channels;
- use two active channels at alpha creation;
- unlock a new channel through sustained self-chosen effort;
- run an Artificer-like universal converter without free power;
- apply alignment restrictions without overriding intent;
- use land cards and cardless sources interchangeably in operation shape;
- receive, refuse, trade, archive, or repair land-based loot;
- run a GM land deck without bypassing the world's physics.
