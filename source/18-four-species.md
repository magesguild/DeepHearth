# The Four Species

## Alchemist

```text
Body: +1   Sense: +2   Craft: +3   Will: +0
Integrity: 3   Reserve: 3
```

The Alchemist senses material state and transforms inputs into temporary
capabilities, tools, and environmental changes.

Starting kit:

```text
portable laboratory
2 reagents
empty vial
formula book
one light tool
```

Signature actions:

- **Prepare:** turn a declared reagent into a prepared extract;
- **Analyze:** identify a public property without inventing hidden meaning;
- **Catalyze:** spend an extract to alter a declared state;
- **Quarantine:** contain a dangerous process;
- **Revise:** change a formula at Rest and record why.

Boundary: the Alchemist does not consume an offered sample without declaring
custody and permission.

Rest practice: observe, prepare, test, spend, record, revise.

## Wayfinder

```text
Body: +1   Sense: +3   Craft: +0   Will: +2
Integrity: 3   Reserve: 3
```

The Wayfinder senses routes, timing, traces, boundaries, and return conditions.

Starting kit:

```text
route thread
map board
chalk or marker
small light
one compass-like instrument
```

Signature actions:

- **Read Trace:** distinguish visible relation from unknown meaning;
- **Mark Route:** preserve a path without claiming its destination;
- **Time Passage:** identify latency and danger before movement;
- **Find Return:** establish a legal recovery path;
- **Guide:** share route knowledge while preserving private boundaries.

Boundary: the Wayfinder never reports an unknown destination as known.

Rest practice: mark, compare, preserve, and return.

## Bastion

```text
Body: +3   Sense: +0   Craft: +1   Will: +2
Integrity: 4   Reserve: 2
```

The Bastion senses force, structural pressure, boundaries, and what can be held
without breaking.

Starting kit:

```text
shield
protective layer
anchor cord
repair wedge
one boundary marker
```

Signature actions:

- **Guard:** intercept pressure for a declared body or object;
- **Anchor:** make a position harder to move;
- **Shelter:** create a temporary safe receiving state;
- **Redirect:** turn force toward terrain or an available route;
- **Hold:** preserve a boundary while spending cycles and Reserve.

Boundary: the Bastion cannot protect everything at once. It must declare what
receives protection.

Rest practice: inspect load, repair anchor, release pressure, shelter.

## Hearthkeeper

```text
Body: +0   Sense: +1   Craft: +2   Will: +3
Integrity: 3   Reserve: 4
```

The Hearthkeeper senses conditions, relationships, recovery paths, permission,
and what allows a group to return.

Starting kit:

```text
hearth kit
3 portions of care material
rest cloth or shelter marker
memory ledger
one signal instrument
```

Signature actions:

- **Tend:** reduce a named condition through a declared input;
- **Receive:** create a safe state for a body, packet, or confession;
- **Reconcile:** make a damaged relationship's next legal action visible;
- **Keep Watch:** preserve a group's return path during Rest;
- **Call Home:** invite a body without compelling its arrival.

Boundary: the Hearthkeeper cannot consent for another body.

Rest practice: receive, tend, remember, forgive or release, Rest.

## Hybrid growth

These species are starting membranes, not prisons. A character may graft a word,
organ, relationship, or practice from another species when play provides a
witnessed reason and a cost.

The new membrane must declare what it senses, consumes, produces, protects,
refuses, and how it rests.
