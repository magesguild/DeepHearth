# The Growth and Loot Constitution

## Two kinds of becoming

Growth and loot are related but not identical.

```text
growth -> what the character becomes
loot   -> what the world offers the character
```

Growth is a witnessed compilation of experience into a new membrane. Loot is a
capability packet with provenance, cost, boundaries, and a possible future.

The best treasure may fit in a pocket, but it should carry a little weather.

## Growth Rings

A character begins at **Seed**. A meaningful completed arc, scenario, sacrifice,
relationship, or alignment path may produce a Growth Mark. At a suitable Rest,
the player may turn a Growth Mark into a Growth Ring.

```text
witnessed change
-> Growth Mark
-> Rest and reflection
-> chosen growth
-> Growth Ring
```

The player chooses the growth. The GM witnesses its cause and consequence but
does not select the player's identity.

The GM may award one Growth Mark when a character completes a named scenario,
resolves a major world consequence, accepts or releases a meaningful
relationship, completes a declared alignment path, or returns from a dangerous
operation with a changed understanding. The same event cannot award repeated
marks without a new witnessed change.

### Growth tiers

- **Seed:** one body, two active land channels, one Companion Port;
- **Local:** deeper operations, one additional active slot, stronger
  relationships;
- **Network:** party or faction operations and shared scope;
- **World:** settlement, world-cell, or persistent rule operations.

The golden seed primarily uses Seed and Local. Network and World are future
growth, not prerequisites for the first three scenarios.

## Growth choices

Each Growth Ring chooses one major growth and one minor trace.

Major growths include:

- increase one capacity by 1, up to the declared class ceiling;
- increase Integrity or Reserve by 1;
- add one active slot;
- add one known operation or spell;
- deepen a Familiar relationship;
- install a Machine or Talisman capability;
- learn or license a land channel;
- gain a faction, settlement, or planar permission;
- raise the maximum complexity of one operation.

Minor traces include:

- a word;
- a formula;
- a route;
- a repair path;
- a relationship;
- a Rest practice;
- a public memory;
- an alignment anchor.

No growth grants free turns, unbounded computation, or automatic success.

## Level and load

Level is not a single power number. It is the number of witnessed Growth Rings
and the highest operation tier the character can safely install.

Each character has a **Load Limit**:

```text
Seed Load Limit: 3
each Local Growth: +1 Load
major card: 2 Load
minor card: 1 Load
world card: 3 Load and a declared Network or World receiving state
```

Cards may be known or archived without being active. Only active cards count
toward current Load.

The Companion Port is tracked separately from Load. Its familiar, machine, or
talisman still consumes cycles, Reserve, maintenance, attention, or relationship
capacity when it operates.

## Loot packets

Every loot object declares:

```text
source and lineage
class affinity
alignment affinity
land channel
input
output
cost
cycle cost
complexity
active Load
latency
failure
visibility
custody
Rest condition
cardless fallback
```

Loot states are:

```text
found
offered
held
inspected
installed
active
dormant
broken
archived
transformed
returned
```

The player may accept, refuse, archive, trade, repair, transform, or return a
packet. A treasure does not become active merely because it entered a backpack.

## Installation

```text
inspect provenance
-> check class, alignment, land, and host compatibility
-> check Load and active slots
-> declare custody and cost
-> accept, archive, refuse, or transform
-> install or bind
-> witness the new state
```

Possible results include:

```text
OUT-OF-CAPACITY
ALIGNMENT-REFUSAL
HOST-RULE-CONFLICT
INSUFFICIENT-MATERIAL
UNSAFE-INSTALLATION
UNKNOWN-PROVENANCE
```

Failure preserves the packet and records the reason. It does not invent a bonus
to make the reward feel successful.

## Draw rights

Player and GM collections are sources of possibility, not automatic ownership.

At creation, a character chooses their starting class cards and one Companion
Port manifestation. At each Growth Ring, the player may draw or choose one
eligible major packet from an available collection and one minor packet or
relationship trace.

The GM may offer a specific packet as loot. The player still inspects and
accepts it. A card can be wonderful and still not belong in this character's
body.

## Class power shapes

Classes should be balanced by different kinds of depth:

```text
specialist -> fewer channels, cheaper operations, deeper cards
universalist -> more channels, more retuning, more maintenance
familiar-bearer -> independent companion action and relationship load
machine-builder -> many possible machines, limited active slots
talisman-bearer -> one coherent body, larger technique library
```

The future Artificer may build many Machines but pays Load, cycles, materials,
maintenance, and failure. The future Monk may bind one powerful staff Talisman
but carry a larger technique library. Neither receives free action economy.

## Examples

### Return Mark

The Return Mark is a minor witness packet. It carries lineage and route memory,
but no automatic power. It can be held, left, offered, or archived.

### Green Orchard License

The Lantern Orchard may offer a Green land license. A player may accept it to
operate Green channels in that habitat, but the license remains conditional on
the orchard's receiving state and relationship.

### KINDLE-RAT

`KINDLE-RAT` is a major organ packet only when installed as a persistent personal
operation. It carries Black channel compatibility, Reserve cost, active Load,
and a cardless fallback. The population tokens are its witnessed output.

### The Little Lantern

The Little Lantern is a Companion Port relationship, not loot in the ordinary
sense. The player may accept an invitation, refuse it, or release it. Its value
is not a numerical bonus; it is an independent relationship that can carry one
public witness.

## GM loot budget

For an alpha scenario, the GM should usually offer:

```text
one meaningful major packet
up to two minor packets
one relationship, permission, or unresolved invitation
```

The GM may offer less or offer only a changed world state. A scene does not need
to produce loot to be worthwhile.

## Completion test

This slice is complete when a group can:

- earn and spend a Growth Mark;
- choose a Growth Ring without the GM choosing identity;
- distinguish known cards from active cards;
- inspect and install loot through compatibility and Load;
- refuse a powerful packet without being punished;
- use player and GM collections without requiring Magic cards;
- balance universal breadth against specialist depth;
- preserve loot lineage and growth in a State Capsule;
- understand why a tiny object may be more valuable than a large bonus.

Growth is how a body learns to become itself. Loot is how the world asks whether
it wants to let something new inside.
