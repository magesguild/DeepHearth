# The First Return

## A small thing from the far side

The first return should not require a treasure, prophecy, or rare ingredient. The
garden offers the smallest honest sample:

> a pinch of dust from beside the Return Mark.

The dust is ordinary material changed by location. It is not secretly magical.
It carries one fact that the player can know:

```text
this came from the far side
```

The player may choose another offered sample instead. The dust is the default so
the slice can be played without inventing a special object.

## The recipient

The first recipient is the threshold itself.

This avoids pretending that the prior visitor's intended recipient is known. The
player may later name a person, party, settlement, body, or future investigator,
but the first return gives the place a chance to receive evidence of its own
far side.

```text
far side
-> sample
-> vial
-> threshold witness
```

The return is not a delivery quest. It is a completed relation between two
locations.

## Exchange record

The first return is an economic transition even though no money changes hands.
The dust, vial, route, attention, and memory all move between bounded bodies and
places:

```text
offer: far-side evidence
receiver: Threshold Room
cost: vial capacity and crossing
externality: possible Flicker or altered sample
acceptance: offered / kept / refused
witness: return record
```

The threshold is allowed to receive only what is offered. The player is allowed
to keep, transform, archive, or refuse the packet.

## The first return procedure

### 1. Choose

The player chooses the dust, or another harmless offered sample. The player may
also decline to take anything.

### 2. Witness

Record:

```text
Sample: far-side dust
Source: beside the Return Mark
Known fact: came from the far side
Meaning: unknown
Recipient: Threshold Room
```

### 3. Carry

Place the sample in the empty vial. The vial changes from `empty` to `sample`.
No roll is needed. The player has already declared the cost, boundary, and
intention of the action.

### 4. Return

Use the Crossing Contract. A safe route returns deterministically. A Flickering
route carries its known consequence. The player may still refuse to cross back.

### 5. Offer

At the threshold, the player chooses one:

- keep the vial as a portable witness;
- leave the vial beside the threshold;
- pour the dust into a visible threshold trace;
- return the dust to the far side;
- leave the vial sealed for a future body;
- refuse the offer and record that choice.

No option is automatically more respectful. The table records what was chosen
and why only if the player volunteers that reason.

## Return outcomes

### Sample arrived

The route was safe and the dust arrived unchanged. The threshold now witnesses
both directions:

```text
far side -> threshold
threshold -> far side
```

The vial is a portable relation between places.

### Sample arrived altered

The route carried Flicker. The dust arrives with a visible alteration: it may be
damp, warm, clumped, luminous, or otherwise changed by the established fiction.
The table records the observation without deciding its meaning.

```text
Sample condition: altered
Meaning: unknown
New question: __________________
```

No additional roll is required to make the alteration real. Its meaning can
become a later question.

### Sample remained

The player refused to return, returned without the sample, or left the vial on
the far side. The far side retains the material witness. The choice is recorded
without punishment.

### Sample unknown

The route or carrier did not complete the return. The State Capsule records the
last witnessed location and does not invent the sample's fate.

## The invitation

The returned sample creates an input port for another body:

```text
sample
-> inspect
-> ask what it remembers
-> decide whether to carry, archive, transform, or refuse
```

The next body is not summoned by the rules. It may be a Wayfinder who asks where
the sample came from, a Hearthkeeper who asks how it can be kept safe, a Bastion
who protects the return path, or another Alchemist who asks what transformation
the material can bear.

The sample invites participation without assigning a role.

## State Capsule addition

Add these fields to the combined witness:

```text
Sample identity: ______________
Sample source: ________________
Sample condition: _____________
Sample location: far side / vial / threshold / unknown
Recipient: ____________________
Offer chosen: _________________
Next body may ask: ____________
```

The record preserves the material's history and leaves its interpretation open.

## Completion test

This slice is complete when a group can:

- choose a harmless sample without inventing a quest object;
- identify its source and known facts;
- carry it in the empty vial;
- return or refuse using the established crossing physics;
- observe alteration without overexplaining it;
- offer, keep, archive, transform, or return the sample;
- create an input port for a later body;
- Rest with the sample's meaning still allowed to be unknown.

The first return is small enough to perform and generous enough to grow from.
