# Reference Body

## Deep Hearth as CP/M 2

Deep Hearth should be:

- small enough to understand completely;
- bounded enough to run by hand;
- rich enough to demonstrate memory, resources, cells, encounters, and Rest;
- modular enough to grow through new words and organisms;
- inspectable enough to replay and repair;
- joyful enough to invite inhabitation rather than merely demonstrate rules.

It is the living reference body for Basin. The abstractions are not complete
until people can use them at a table.

## Bare-metal distillation

The bare-metal candidate is not a rewrite made from guesses. It is a reduction
earned by observing what Deep Hearth actually needs in order to remain a game, a
computer, and a world that can return.

```text
Deep Hearth
-> identify irreducible state
-> identify irreducible transitions
-> identify the smallest memory surface
-> identify the smallest input/output membrane
-> remove setting and presentation dependencies
-> preserve the witness and Rest contracts
-> boot the Basin kernel on a minimal substrate
```

## Talisman

> Every class has a membrane. Every party develops a membrane. The world is the
> garden in which the party grows.

> Every encounter is a program with laws, not a script with one permitted ending.

> Randomness enters through a physical source. Agency enters through a real
> choice among legal actions. Emergence is what happens when those meet a world
> that remembers.

> A game may contain another computer. Let it compute one bounded thing, show its
> work through a witness, and return its answer to the world.

> A Game Master designs the physics and tends the garden. Players decide what to
> do inside it. No one is required to erase their own boundary in order to make
> the organism whole.

## The open kernel

The generic Basin core is:

```text
state
-> cells
-> membranes
-> resources
-> clocks
-> events
-> agency
-> entropy
-> memory
-> witness
-> Rest
```

Everything else is an adapter or a content layer. Deep Hearth is the first body
in which the kernel will become legible.
