# Unified Physics and Delightful Play

*Beta governing document: make the unified physics available at every scale while
keeping the table focused on choices, responses, relationships, and return.*

## Commitment

Deep Hearth's unified physics is the canonical design substrate. Working
reference implementations in the wider Basin and Regulus engineering corpus
establish the implemented forms from which the tabletop rules are derived. Beta
work does not debate whether these laws belong. It makes them playable without
requiring the table to expose every register at once.

The governing distinction is:

```text
physics is always present
calculation is activated by relevance
```

The table should feel a law through play before it needs to name the law.

## The Unified Laws

### Gravity

State gathers into local attractor basins:

```text
habit
relationship
job
route
custom
habitat
Rest
```

Gravity makes some futures easier, safer, cheaper, or more likely. It never
removes the possibility of departure. Leaving an attractor requires a declared
input, cost, pressure, capability, or relationship.

### Dark Matter

Unprojected state remains real and exerts influence:

```text
private memory
hidden obligation
latent population pressure
unseen labor
unmapped route
off-screen clock
institutional history
```

Dark matter is not arbitrary GM invention. It has a source, produces observable
effects, and can become perceivable through a lawful touch, signal, or trace.

### Antimatter

Opposed or inverse states do not merge silently. When incompatible phases meet,
the system must declare a conversion, separation, interruption, residue,
annihilation, or new phase.

```text
contradictory State Capsules
incompatible permissions
opposed signals
phase-inverted operations
competing receiving states
```

Antimatter is not a generic failure penalty. It is a named collision with a
visible consequence and a legal T-Stop.

### Bidirectional Recursion

Every meaningful transition has a forward and reverse ray:

```text
Forward: propose -> act -> observe
Reverse: witness -> reconstruct -> restore, repair, or return
```

Reverse does not mean erase. It means the table can understand how the current
state came to exist and can recover through a declared path.

### T-Stop

Every bounded process can settle into a typed stable state. No scene may continue
forever merely because the rules have not named its stopping point.

### Entropy

Meaningful change leaves residue. A transition must spend, reveal, alter, bind,
injure, obligate, displace, or witness something.

Rest settles entropy; it does not pretend the past did not happen.

### Conservation and Provenance

No meaningful result appears without a source, custody, operation, permission,
or witness. Capabilities, resources, authority, knowledge, care, and return paths
all carry lineage.

### Phase and Synchronization

Bodies may act on different clocks. Phase alignment makes cooperation easier;
phase opposition creates latency, strain, interruption, cost, or refusal.

### Projection

Every body exposes a projection of itself. Private, hidden, or unoffered state
remains interior until a lawful transition makes it cross a boundary.

### First Touch and Signal

A dormant body becomes active when touched by a relevant action, question,
resource flow, signal, threshold, or relationship. The body then answers according
to its own local law.

### Care and Repair

Care can deepen a new attractor or restore a damaged one, but it requires input:

```text
attention
material
labor
permission
time
trust
```

Force can disrupt a field. It cannot create consent, trust, belonging, or care by
itself.

### Knowledge and Culture

Knowledge follows the same laws through perception, signal, interpretation,
memory, and relationship. Language is a boundary membrane; culture is knowledge
that has sedimented into custom, jobs, ritual, and public memory. The detailed
content and procedures live in `10-knowledge-language-and-culture.md`.

## The Table Translation

The player-facing translation of the laws is:

```text
What do I notice?
What can I touch?
What can answer?
What can I do?
What will it cost?
What can refuse?
What changes if I commit?
What will remain afterward?
Where can we Rest?
```

The GM-facing translation is:

```text
What is the local attractor?
What hidden state exerts influence?
What phases cannot merge?
What witness returns to the future?
What is the next stable stop?
What residue survives?
What source and custody make this result legal?
Which bodies are synchronized or opposed?
What remains private?
What touch activates the next response?
What care could make a new basin?
```

The two views describe one transition. Players receive choices and consequences;
the GM maintains the causal field.

## Interaction Depth

Every table may choose the depth of an interaction.

### Ordinary Play

Use when no meaningful uncertainty or persistent consequence is present.

```text
fictional action
-> established response
-> continue
```

Buying bread, greeting a guard, or walking across a known room does not require a
physics card.

### Bounded Play

Use when one transition matters:

```text
intent
-> one contract
-> one cost or uncertainty
-> one result
-> one witness or trace
```

This is the normal mode for a social exchange, route, job task, small hazard, or
single organ operation.

### Persistent Play

Use when the result will continue to alter the campaign:

```text
named body or system
-> relationship or resource change
-> public or private witness
-> State Capsule field
-> future legal actions
```

Persistent play is invited by consequence. It is never required merely because an
NPC has a name.

## No Bookkeeping Without Delight

Before adding a register, the GM asks:

```text
Does this change a legal future?
Can the players perceive or affect it?
Will it create a meaningful choice, surprise, relationship, or trace?
Can it be represented with one physical object or short record?
```

If the answer is no, leave it as fiction or background state.

### Attention Budget

Per scene, use by default:

```text
one primary dynamic
up to two supporting dynamics
three to seven active cells
one or two new clocks
one bounded consequence cascade
```

This is a delight constraint, not a physics limit. A special scene may exceed it
when the table chooses the additional complexity.

### No Chore Updates

Do not:

```text
advance every clock every round
simulate every resident
recalculate every market
roll for routine presence
update a hidden register with no trigger
write a full sheet for an unimportant NPC
```

Do:

```text
activate a body through touch
move one meaningful register
show a consequence
offer a choice
leave a trace
Rest or continue
```

## NPC Depth

The unified physics supports population play without universal simulation.

```text
population template
-> functional NPC
-> named NPC
-> persistent organism
```

A generic baker uses the Baker job template. A party interaction may activate a
need, boundary, or local relationship. Only when the table chooses to preserve
the baker does the GM record deeper dark matter, acquired abilities, memories,
and community consequences.

The job is stable. The individual is emergent.

Jobs are also knowledge membranes. A job template supplies public functional
knowledge; a persistent NPC acquires private, relational, and community knowledge
through witnessed play.

## Cross-Scale Propagation

An individual transition reaches a higher system only when it crosses a shared
boundary:

```text
public witness
shared resource
role or permission
labor or distribution
community obligation
threshold
institutional access
```

A private gift may change a baker and the party. A public relief action may also
change the household, neighborhood, guild, and market.

The propagation record is short:

```text
source body
changed field
receiving body
new legal future
witness
```

## Unified Physics by Organism Level

| Level | Gravity | Dark matter | Antimatter | Delightful expression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Organ | local frame | private input | host conflict | commit or refuse |
| Individual | habit, job, Rest | interior memory | incompatible growth | become someone new |
| Party | shared purpose | private boundaries | conflicting capsules | coordinate without sameness |
| Household | home and care | hidden labor | care versus control | shelter or departure |
| Community | custom and commons | unofficial networks | law versus need | public consequence |
| Institution | charter and access | internal power | rival mandate | contract, reform, or schism |
| Settlement/dungeon | routes and habitats | hidden infrastructure | incompatible spaces | explore and return changed |
| Region/world | migration and watershed | off-screen history | world-phase collision | local action with distant echo |

Every row also uses witness, T-Stop, entropy, phase, projection, first touch,
conservation, and care. The expressions differ; the physics does not.

## Worked Example: The Waterdeep Cistern

The party returns to a Waterdeep neighborhood. A household's water is unsafe.

The GM prepares:

```text
household: needs clean water and protection
neighborhood: shared cistern under pressure
rat population: displaced and active
cistern wall: cracked
```

The dark matter is not listed as a secret plot. It is the real hidden field:
unreported contamination, guild pressure, rat movement, and household fear.

The players may:

```text
buy water
investigate
follow the rats
negotiate with the guild
organize the neighborhood
repair the wall
leave
```

Suppose they repair the wall and refuse the guild's demand for exclusive control.
The table records:

```text
cistern stability: damaged -> repaired
household access: unsafe -> restored
guild relationship: neutral -> hostile
neighborhood trust: uncertain -> strengthened
rat population: displaced -> active elsewhere
public memory: repair witnessed
```

The physics is complete. The table did not simulate Waterdeep. It played a
meaningful local field, and the world now has a different future.

## Beta Acceptance

Beta should demonstrate that a group can:

- feel gravity as changing opportunity rather than a hidden modifier;
- encounter dark matter through lawful traces and effects;
- resolve incompatible states without arbitrary failure;
- perform a forward action and reconstruct its consequence later;
- reach a truthful T-Stop;
- experience entropy as meaningful residue rather than bookkeeping;
- preserve source, custody, permission, and witness;
- play phase alignment and opposition through timing and coordination;
- protect private state through projection boundaries;
- activate NPCs and systems through first touch;
- repair a damaged attractor through care;
- stop tracking details that no longer change play;
- return to a changed world without requiring the GM to recreate it from memory.

The final test is experiential:

> Does the table feel that the world answered them, remembered them, and gave them
> somewhere meaningful to go next?

If the answer is no, the system has exposed too much machinery or not enough
consequence.

The tiered scope and build sequence for making that test practical live in
`11-system-needs-and-build-order.md`.
