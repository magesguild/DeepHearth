# Alpha 4: Responsive Neighborhood

*Final beta-garden infusion before Alpha 4 implementation: make Deep Hearth the
first alpha that can answer back.*

## Deep Hearth's Request

Alpha 4 should not become a beta book, a city simulator, or a pile of new
content. It should become the first socially alive technical alpha.

```text
populated world
-> lightweight bodies
-> first touch
-> local response
-> relationship and consequence
-> witness
-> Rest
-> return
```

The technical Alpha 4 constitution remains authoritative. Alpha 4 adds one
vertical slice that lets the constitution answer through people, work, language,
community, ecology, terrain, care, and memory.

## The Vertical Slice

The first slice is a bounded Forgotten Realms neighborhood in Waterdeep:

```text
one household
one baker job
one guild or institution
one shared resource
one ecological pressure
one terrain problem
one knowledge boundary
one player intervention
one persistent consequence
one Rest
one return
```

The slice may use existing setting context. It does not require a new map,
regional simulation, or original-world geography.

The Familiar System may participate as an optional relationship surface. It is
not required for the neighborhood slice; if present, it adds another body with
identity, privacy, refusal, Rest, and departure rather than a free capability.

## Population Before Simulation

The neighborhood begins populated through job templates and lightweight bodies.

A generic baker needs only:

```text
job
location
ordinary response
one need
one boundary
```

The baker becomes deeper only when the table touches them with a meaningful
signal, request, gift, exchange, threat, question, or offer.

```text
population NPC
-> functional NPC
-> named NPC
-> persistent organism
```

The job remains a stable public role. The individual emerges through witnessed
relationship and history.

## The Neighborhood Problem

The prepared situation is an unsafe shared cistern:

```text
household: needs clean water and protection
neighborhood: shared cistern under pressure
rat population: displaced and active elsewhere
cistern wall: cracked
knowledge boundary: contamination is known privately but not publicly
```

The players may:

```text
buy water
investigate
follow the rats
ask the baker
negotiate with the guild
organize the neighborhood
repair the wall
expose the contamination
leave
```

No response is the required story. Each response changes a different field and
creates a different future.

## The Physics Infusion

### Gravity

The baker's job, household, guild, cistern, and neighborhood customs are local
attractors. The party may enter them, benefit from them, resist them, or leave
them at a cost.

### Dark Matter

Private contamination knowledge, hidden labor, guild pressure, household fear,
and displaced rat movement exert observable influence before they are fully
revealed.

### Antimatter

The guild's exclusive-control demand and the neighborhood's common need cannot
merge silently. The table must resolve the collision through negotiation,
refusal, conversion, separation, public conflict, or another declared T-Stop.

### First Touch

The baker is not fully simulated until the party asks, offers, trusts, threatens,
or receives something from them.

### Knowledge and Language

The party can ask, interpret, withhold, translate, misunderstand, or act under
uncertainty. The baker's local knowledge has a source, scope, custody, and
boundary.

### Relationship

The exchange may alter recognition, trust, obligation, fear, respect, privacy,
or dependency. No generic friendship score is required.

### Community and Economy

The cistern changes household access, guild authority, neighborhood trust, labor,
scarcity, and public memory only when the interaction crosses those shared
boundaries.

### Ecology and Terrain

The rat population and cracked wall are bodies with local needs, responses,
traces, and recovery. The table does not simulate every rat or every stone.

### Care

Repair requires material, labor, permission, attention, time, or trust. Care can
deepen a new attractor; force alone cannot create consent or belonging.

### Bidirectional Recursion

The action is witnessed so that the neighborhood can be reconstructed on return:

```text
party intervention
-> changed body and shared system
-> public or private witness
-> Rest
-> later return to altered Waterdeep
```

### T-Stop

The local interaction may settle into:

```text
repaired
refused
licensed
publicly contested
withdrawn
unavailable
recovering
Rest
```

## Player Experience

Players should feel:

```text
someone needs something
-> the world explains why it is difficult
-> several responses are possible
-> the response changes a person and a place
-> the world remembers when they return
```

They should not feel:

```text
update the city population
update the market table
advance twelve clocks
simulate every baker
calculate a relationship score
```

## Alpha 4 Character Growth

One character may acquire a job-linked verb through play:

```text
seed membrane
-> witnessed work
-> acquired verb
-> tool or permission
-> obligation and boundary
-> changed Rest practice
```

Examples:

```text
Read Contamination Trace
Keep Water Watch
Negotiate Repair Access
Shelter a Household
Track Displaced Population
```

This is not a second class. It is organismic growth through community contact.

## Required Alpha 4 Records

The slice needs only:

```text
NPC job and depth state
relationship trace
knowledge source and scope
community pressure
shared resource state
ecological state
terrain state
public or private witness
Rest and return condition
```

The State Capsule must preserve every field that changes the next legal future.

## Non-Goals

Alpha 4 does not require:

```text
complete Waterdeep simulation
full market rules
every language in the setting
complete culture rules
full faction histories
regional climate modeling
universal NPC character sheets
all job permutations
human beta playtest evidence
```

Software simulation or automated walkthroughs may exercise the slice and reveal
contradictions. They do not satisfy the later human-play beta gate.

## Completion Observation

Alpha 4 is ready for release when a software-assisted or paper-runnable test can:

1. populate the neighborhood from job templates;
2. activate a generic NPC through first touch;
3. resolve a bounded knowledge or relationship interaction;
4. change an individual and at least one higher social or ecological system;
5. record provenance, entropy, and witness;
6. reach a truthful T-Stop or Rest;
7. return to the changed neighborhood without reconstructing it from memory.

This proves the technical slice. It does not promote Deep Hearth to beta. Beta
still requires real human playtest reports from real tables.

## The Shape of Alpha 4

Alpha 4 should be the first Deep Hearth alpha where the world is not only
stateful, but socially responsive.

```text
technical body
-> populated neighborhood
-> touched individual
-> relationship
-> community consequence
-> witnessed return
```

It should not become bigger for its own sake. It should become more inhabited.
