# Beta Graduation Gate

Deep Hearth graduates from alpha to beta only when the whole body, not merely a
feature list, has earned the transition.

## Required implementation evidence

- Core action, resource, condition, and T-Stop rules are internally consistent.
- Character creation is complete for the supported alpha species.
- At least one combat, one ecological encounter, one spell, one organ, and one
  recovery path are fully playable.
- State Capsules save, restore, branch, and reject stale state coherently.
- Death, incapacitation, surrender, refusal, recovery, and Rest are distinct in
  actual procedures.
- The physical table can use grids, figures, dice, ordinary objects, and forms.
- GM templates can generate new content without designer intervention.
- Player-facing procedures no longer require implementation-level reading.
- Unified physics can be felt through ordinary play and resolved through bounded
  procedures without requiring a full world simulation.
- Persistent NPCs, communities, and systems preserve forward transitions and
  reverse-readable witnesses.

## Required evidence of play

At least a small number of real human groups, including participants who did not
author the rules, must have completed the beta playtest protocol and produced
witnessed reports covering:

- character creation;
- one ordinary exploration session;
- one ecological encounter;
- one combat or dangerous pressure scene;
- one refusal or surrender;
- one injury and recovery;
- one State Capsule stop and return;
- one unexpected but lawful consequence;
- one hidden field becoming perceivable through a lawful trace;
- one incompatible-state collision reaching an explicit T-Stop;
- one care action repairing or deepening an attractor;
- one zoom from an aggregate body into a local interaction and back;
- one partial-knowledge or language-boundary interaction with preserved
  provenance;
- one NPC whose job knowledge becomes deeper through a persistent relationship;
- one vertical Waterdeep slice using a household, job, community, exchange,
  ecological pressure, terrain problem, and knowledge boundary;
- one demonstration that an optional module can remain absent without breaking
  the core session.

Software simulations, automated transcripts, solo author walkthroughs, generated
examples, and internal design review are valuable supplementary evidence. They
cannot satisfy the human-play gate for the beta release.

Each qualifying report records:

```text
play date
rules version
facilitator
participant count and roles
materials used
scenario
observed surprises and failures
bookkeeping burden
what the group would play again
facilitator attestation
```

The evidence need not prove universal balance. It must reveal whether people can
enter, understand, play, stop, return, and extend the world.

## Promotion decision

The team records:

```text
implemented claims
known failures
playtest evidence
unresolved questions
accepted risks
promotion author
promotion date
```

Passing one feature does not promote every attached design claim. The body
graduates only when its central loop is playable without hidden author support.
