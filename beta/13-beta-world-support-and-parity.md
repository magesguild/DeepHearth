# Beta World Support and Publication Parity

*Beta graduation requirement: grow beyond the Responsive Neighborhood Alpha into
a reference body capable of supporting a whole Forgotten Realms-compatible
campaign world.*

## The Gate

Deep Hearth does not graduate from alpha to beta merely because one neighborhood
is playable. Beta requires the rules to support meaningful play across:

```text
individual
party
household
community
town
city
faction and institution
region
world
plane
```

The same Basin physics must remain valid at every level:

```text
state
-> cells
-> membranes
-> resources
-> perception
-> relationships
-> clocks
-> events
-> agency
-> entropy
-> memory
-> witness
-> Rest
```

The table need not simulate every resident, market, or grain of soil. It must be
able to zoom from a local body to a larger system, make a meaningful change, and
return with the consequence preserved.

Beta must also ship at least one complete campaign module. The module is the
bridge between the constitution and an actual world:

```text
campaign premise
starting community or settlement
named characters and jobs
player-facing entry
GM-facing hidden state
maps or spatial surfaces
factions and institutions
ecology and monsters
economy and resources
knowledge and language boundaries
gods, worship, or divine pressures where relevant
scenarios and escalation paths
treasure, growth, and job opportunities
State Capsule and return points
multiple lawful endings
```

It must be runnable without the designer sitting at the table. A module may use
Forgotten Realms as its populated setting layer, provided the publication and IP
boundary is explicit.

## Campaign Conversion Layer

Existing campaign modules do not need to be rewritten to become Deep Hearth
campaigns. They can receive a conversion projection:

```text
existing module
-> host content and scenario
-> job stamps and seed membranes
-> needs, boundaries, and knowledge scope
-> relationships and community roles
-> Basin action and encounter projections
-> T-Stops, witnesses, and State Capsules
```

An existing NPC may receive a job stamp such as:

```text
tavernkeeper -> innkeeper or information broker
guard        -> boundary warden
priest       -> ritual caretaker
merchant     -> exchange factor
wizard       -> licensed organ practitioner
noble        -> patron or authority holder
thief        -> route broker or access specialist
```

An existing creature receives a Deep Hearth body projection:

```text
stat block
-> habitat
-> needs
-> senses
-> boundary
-> legal responses
-> ecological role
-> T-Stop set
-> Rest and recovery
```

An existing deity receives a worship projection:

```text
domain
-> rites and offerings
-> permissions and obligations
-> miracles and refusal
-> temple or community body
-> witness and return path
```

The host module remains the content layer. Deep Hearth supplies state, agency,
jobs, relationships, ecology, memory, and return. A conversion is complete only
when the resulting campaign can run without designer intervention and can produce
Player Guide, GM Guide, and Bestiary projections without copying protected text.

## Planes Before New Physics

Before beta, planes use the same Deep Hearth physics as towns, dungeons, cities,
and worlds. Planar travel is a boundary and transfer procedure, not permission
to invent a separate rules engine.

```text
observe boundary
-> identify plane and receiving state
-> check capability, permission, and host law
-> quote cost and latency
-> accept, refuse, or delay crossing
-> serialize Traveler Capsule
-> cross
-> witness arrival
```

Each plane may have different inhabitants, resources, culture, hazards, gods,
ecology, and content. It may express the same physics with different local
values. A future post-beta expansion may explore planes with genuinely different
physics; that is not a beta requirement.

## Gods and Worship

Beta must support gods, divine powers, worship, temples, rites, clergy,
communities, heresies, vows, offerings, miracles, and divine refusal.

A god is a world-scale cell or relationship body with:

```text
identity and domain
perception boundary
accepted worship or relationship
rites and offerings
permissions and gifts
obligations and taboos
alignment or compatibility response
signs and witnesses
refusal conditions
clerical or community receiving states
Rest, death, afterlife, or return paths where declared
```

Worship is not a passive alignment label. It is a relationship and community
practice:

```text
attention
-> rite or offering
-> divine or institutional response
-> permission, obligation, sign, refusal, or miracle
-> public and private witness
```

Divine power must obey the same boundaries as every other organ or host:

```text
source
-> input
-> cost and latency
-> receiving state
-> effect
-> residue
-> witness
-> T-Stop or Rest
```

A god may refuse. A priest may disagree with a temple. A congregation may change.
The player retains private intent; worship changes what a divine system will
receive or provide.

The Player Guide needs enough information to choose worship, vows, divine
relationships, and meaningful costs. The GM Guide needs divine ecology, temples,
rites, factions, signs, miracles, and refusal procedures.

## Full World Support

Beta support must include procedures or templates for:

```text
towns and settlements
cities and districts
households and communities
guilds and institutions
economies and commons
ecologies and habitats
geology, terrain, and infrastructure
weather and seasons
travel and planar boundaries
languages and knowledge
religion and worship
factions and politics
war, disaster, and population pressure
```

These are not simultaneous simulations. Each receives a lawful interface and
becomes active when it changes a meaningful legal future.

## Robust Monster Manual

The Bestiary must be robust enough to support a campaign without requiring the
designer to invent missing physics at the table.

Every complete creature body declares:

```text
identity and species
habitat
needs and resources
senses and perception ports
capacities or local registers
membranes and capabilities
relationships and social role
boundaries and refusal
legal responses
combat and noncombat actions
ecological role
population or territory state
clocks and triggers
T-Stop set
injury, retreat, surrender, and recovery
Rest condition
loot, traces, or residues
visibility boundary
State Capsule fields
```

The Bestiary must support:

```text
ordinary encounter
social encounter
ecological encounter
combat
negotiation
population play
habitat change
growth and transformation
```

The complete body belongs in the GM-facing Monster Manual. Players receive only
what their characters can perceive, infer, learn, or be told. Hidden state must
remain lawful rather than arbitrary.

## Publication Separation

Beta graduation requires three usable projections over one constitution.

### Player Guide

The Player Guide must contain:

```text
character creation
seed membranes
jobs and organismic growth
actions and capacities
resources and conditions
relationships and refusal
knowledge and language at the player boundary
travel and exploration
equipment, magic, worship, and familiars
combat and recovery
Rest, T-Stops, death, and return
```

Players should be able to enter a town, dungeon, community, divine relationship,
or encounter without carrying GM bookkeeping or hidden creature implementation.

### GM Guide

The GM Guide must contain:

```text
world and settlement construction
community and economy procedures
ecology and habitat construction
town, city, faction, and institution templates
jobs and NPC depth escalation
knowledge, language, and cultural boundaries
gods, temples, worship, rites, and miracles
planar travel and boundary procedures
encounter programs
clocks, registers, and away-time
State Capsules, branches, and recovery
lawful improvisation
```

### Monster Manual / Bestiary

The Monster Manual must contain complete creature bodies, including the hidden
state and ecological procedures the GM needs to run them honestly.

The three books must agree about:

```text
state
cost
agency
perception
boundary
T-Stop
witness
Rest
recovery
provenance
```

They may reveal different projections. They may not describe contradictory
physics.

## Forgotten Realms and d20/OGL Parity

The beta target is a Basin ruleset capable of supporting a private or properly
licensed Forgotten Realms campaign with as much familiar d20 play parity as the
open constitution can responsibly provide.

Parity means the table can support the expected families of play:

```text
character creation
species and class-like identities
jobs and backgrounds
ability-based action resolution
equipment and treasure
spells and divine powers
combat and conditions
travel and exploration
factions and settlements
monsters and encounters
gods and worship
planes and return paths
campaign continuity
```

Parity does not require copying protected setting text, proprietary presentation,
or another publisher's implementation. Public releases must preserve the
separation between original Basin mechanics and Forgotten Realms Product
Identity, Open Game Content, trademarks, copyright, and attribution obligations.

The compatibility test is practical:

> Can a GM run a familiar d20-compatible Forgotten Realms campaign through the
> Deep Hearth constitution without losing agency, world memory, creature ecology,
> divine relationships, or the ability to stop and return?

## Beta Graduation Evidence

Before the project labels a release Beta, real human playtest reports must cover:

- a personal character and party;
- a household or community;
- a town or settlement;
- a city or district;
- a faction or institution;
- an economy or shared resource;
- an ecology and robust creature encounter;
- a god, temple, worship relationship, or divine refusal;
- planar travel using the shared physics;
- a Monster Manual body run by a GM;
- Player Guide procedures used without GM implementation support;
- a State Capsule stop, return, and consequence;
- an unexpected but lawful world-scale or cross-scale result.

Software simulations, generated transcripts, and internal walkthroughs remain
valuable design evidence. They cannot replace reports from real human tables for
the Beta label.

## Build Sequence

The existing Responsive Neighborhood Alpha is the local seed. Beta grows outward:

```text
neighborhood
-> town
-> city district
-> faction and institution
-> region and world
-> planes using shared physics
-> gods and worship across communities
-> robust Bestiary and role-separated books
-> human beta playtest
```

The world grows by adding lawful bodies and projections, not by turning every
scale into continuous bookkeeping.
