# Knowledge, Language, and Culture

*Beta design and implementation document: make meaning a living substrate flow
without turning conversation into a universal skill check or lore database.*

## Scope

Knowledge in Deep Hearth is not a database that characters query. It is a flow
across membranes:

```text
world signal
-> perception port
-> interpretation
-> memory
-> language or sign
-> relationship
-> shared or private state
-> changed legal future
```

The first playable content may use Forgotten Realms languages, guild cants,
temple phrases, regional dialects, noble titles, faction codes, and
Undermountain markings. The rules remain portable. Athanor's real-language and
living-language work can later provide a deeper original-world content layer.

## Knowledge as a Cell

Every knowledge body that matters to play declares:

```text
source
signal
perception port
scope
custody
interpretation
knowledge state
visibility
relationships
memory location
legal uses
boundary
witness
```

The important question is not only whether a fact is true. It is:

```text
How did this become known?
To whom?
Through which body?
With what authority?
Under what conditions?
```

## Knowledge States

Use the smallest state that changes a legal future:

```text
UNKNOWN       no established result
SENSED        a signal was perceived
OBSERVED      a property was directly witnessed
INFERRED      a conclusion was constructed
TAUGHT        another body offered it
SHARED        it crossed into a receiving group
TRUSTED       a relationship or institution accepts it
CONTESTED     bodies preserve incompatible claims
PRIVATE       it remains inside its current boundary
FORGOTTEN     it exists historically but is not currently recoverable
```

These are not universal grades of truth. They describe provenance, custody,
visibility, and current action space.

## Language as a Membrane

Language is the membrane between bodies:

```text
speaker
-> signal
-> shared or partial vocabulary
-> listener interpretation
-> response
-> relationship change
```

Language carries more than nouns:

```text
authority
register
intimacy
ritual
class
region
history
refusal
humor
threat
```

The same phrase may have different legal effects depending on who speaks, where,
to whom, and under which relationship.

## Unified Physics

### Gravity

Meanings become attractors through repeated use:

```text
word
-> repeated use
-> shared expectation
-> social permission
-> institution or custom
```

A place-name, oath, title, greeting, or job term gains force because a community
has sedimented around it.

### Dark Matter

Linguistic dark matter includes:

```text
unspoken context
private meaning
lost vocabulary
accent and register
inside jokes
taboo terms
untranslated archives
silent signals
```

It has a source and exerts observable effects. An NPC may hesitate at a name, a
guild may refuse a phrase, or a household may recognize a greeting the party does
not understand.

### Antimatter

Language can produce opposed states:

```text
contradictory translation
false cognate
counter-oath
conflicting authority
incompatible name
hostile signal
```

The result must be explicit:

```text
clarification
refusal
misinterpretation
phase shift
social rupture
new shared meaning
```

A translation cannot silently make incompatible concepts identical.

### Bidirectional Recursion

Conversation has a forward and reverse ray:

```text
offer signal
-> receive interpretation
-> answer
-> witness what was meant and understood
```

When misunderstanding occurs, repair traces the divergence, identifies the
source of the mismatch, and creates a new shared meaning. The old
misunderstanding remains part of the relationship history.

### T-Stop

A knowledge interaction may settle into:

```text
ANSWERED
REFUSED
UNKNOWN
CONTESTED
DELAYED
MISUNDERSTOOD
TRANSLATED
WITNESSED
REST
```

Conversation does not require endless social rolls after it reaches a truthful
state.

### Entropy and Conservation

Knowledge leaves residue:

```text
promise
rumor
insult
name
vow
secret
public statement
misunderstanding
```

Every meaningful claim has a source, and a statement can change a relationship
even when its intended meaning was not received.

### Projection

Every speaker offers a projection, not their complete interior:

```text
what is said
what is implied
what is withheld
what the listener can perceive
what the relationship permits
```

Privacy is structural. It is not a secret the GM may reveal arbitrarily.

### First Touch and Signal

A greeting, name, question, signal, look, inscription, or gesture is a directed
touch:

```text
signal
-> body answers, refuses, misreads, or remains silent
```

A generic NPC becomes mechanically active when the party touches them with a
meaningful signal.

## Knowledge Across the Organism Chain

### Capability

A capability is a local interpretation organ:

```text
input
-> recognized pattern
-> legal operation
-> output
```

An Alchemist recognizes material state. A Wayfinder recognizes traces. A
Hearthkeeper recognizes conditions and permission. A Bastion recognizes
structural pressure. No capability sees everything.

### Individual

An individual carries:

```text
private memory
known language
learned vocabulary
trusted sources
misunderstandings
unresolved questions
```

Growth may add a word, route, formula, translation, or perception port.

### Party

Party knowledge contains:

```text
shared Memory
private memories
offered information
unresolved interpretations
team words
```

One character may know something without the party knowing it. Sharing is an
action with scope, custody, and consequence.

### Household

Household knowledge is often tacit:

```text
who knows where things are
who can speak for whom
which visitor is safe
what story may be told outside
which ritual means welcome
```

The party may receive hospitality without receiving the household's private
history.

### Community

Community knowledge becomes:

```text
custom
dialect
public memory
job training
ritual language
rumor
common route
shared warning
```

Shared meaning is a gravitational basin. It can change through public witness,
repeated use, teaching, or disruption.

### Institution

Institutions stabilize knowledge through:

```text
credentials
archives
titles
procedures
laws
secret orders
recognized terminology
```

Institutional knowledge may be authoritative without being complete. An
institution can preserve a map while hiding why a road is forbidden.

### Settlement and Dungeon

A place stores knowledge materially:

```text
graffiti
ruins
locks
road wear
burn marks
missing tools
changed patrols
buried rooms
```

Players learn the dungeon through traces, not only exposition.

### Region and World

Large-scale knowledge includes:

```text
legends
languages
maps
religions
historical trauma
migration memory
cosmology
```

The region does not need to be fully known to influence local play. A rumor may
have gravitational force before its source is reached.

## Knowledge and Jobs

A job is a knowledge membrane.

```text
baker       -> flour routes, hunger, market rhythm, local obligations
guard       -> patrol timing, boundary signals, recognized names
ferryman    -> water behavior, safe crossings, seasonal changes
priest      -> ritual phrases, receiving conditions, public memory
guide       -> routes, landmarks, hazards, return conditions
```

The job template provides public functional knowledge. Individual relationships
reveal deeper, unique knowledge.

## Knowledge and Culture

Culture is knowledge sedimented into bodies and institutions:

```text
repeated signal
-> shared interpretation
-> custom
-> expectation
-> permission or refusal
```

Culture is not an isolated modifier layer. It is accumulated gravity from:

```text
language
memory
jobs
ritual
economy
relationships
care
power
```

## Player Procedure

The player-facing procedure is:

```text
notice a signal
-> say what the character thinks it means
-> choose whether to ask, answer, offer, conceal, translate, or act
-> resolve only if interpretation changes the future
-> record resulting knowledge or relationship
```

The player does not roll merely to be allowed to participate. Uncertainty asks:

```text
Can I interpret this?
Will they answer?
Do I trust the source?
What happens if I repeat it?
What boundary does this word cross?
```

## Forgotten Realms Content

The first campaign may use Common, regional speech, guild cant, temple formulas,
noble titles, faction codes, and Undermountain markings. Deep Hearth does not need
to define every language before play. The substrate rules remain generic while
the setting supplies the language content.

A Waterdeep encounter may involve:

```text
Common speech
a guild sign
a household honorific
a temple formula
a private name
a warning carved into stone
```

The party needs to experience what it can perceive, what is withheld, and what
its response changes.

## Materials and Records

Knowledge may be represented by:

```text
spoken phrase      -> transient signal
card or slip       -> portable offer or vocabulary
ledger             -> durable archive
map                -> spatial projection
name token         -> identity and access pointer
ritual object      -> repeated shared transition
State Capsule      -> witnessed memory and provenance
```

No material exposes more than its declared projection. A written archive may be
durable and still be incomplete; a spoken phrase may be private and still alter
a relationship.

## NPC and Community Depth

Generic NPCs use job knowledge and ordinary responses. A named NPC gains deeper
knowledge state when the table touches them with a consequential exchange.

Persistent knowledge may record:

```text
source and lineage
private or public scope
trusted and contested claims
languages and registers
relationships
promises and secrets
last witness
```

The table chooses whether a piece of knowledge becomes persistent. A town does
not require a complete cultural database to feel populated.

## Beta Implementation Slices

Implement vertically:

1. Give one Waterdeep NPC a public job vocabulary, one private boundary, and one
   local fact.
2. Let a player ask, offer, misunderstand, or refuse.
3. Record the knowledge state and relationship change.
4. Let one result affect a household or community.
5. Repeat with an inscription, route marker, or faction archive in Undermountain.
6. Add one language boundary or partial-comprehension situation.
7. Restore the scene from a State Capsule and preserve the knowledge provenance.

## Completion Observation

This slice is ready to promote when a group can encounter an NPC or place whose
knowledge is partial and situated, make a meaningful choice about asking,
offering, withholding, translating, or acting, and later find that the result has
changed both the relationship and the world without requiring a universal lore
database.
