# Beta Goals

Beta work is developed inside alpha releases. These are goals, not promises that
the current manual already implements them.

## 1. Player-facing clarity

Translate the technical constitution into procedures a new player can use:

```text
what can I do?
what does it cost?
what may refuse?
what changes?
where can I Rest?
```

Acceptance:

- a new player can create a character without reading implementation essays;
- routine, uncertain, opposed, costly, and refused actions are distinguishable;
- T-Stop and death choices are understandable without computer-science vocabulary;
- player materials do not require GM state, hidden state, or event-schema work.

## 2. GM usability

Give the GM compact tools for tending a world rather than scripting outcomes:

- encounter construction;
- ecology and creature templates;
- T-Stop sets;
- clocks and registers;
- hidden-state boundaries;
- resource and consequence tracking;
- State Capsules and branch recovery;
- examples of lawful surprise.

Acceptance:

- a GM can create a room, creature, faction, hazard, or machine from a template;
- every template declares inputs, costs, legal responses, T-Stops, Rest, and traces;
- the GM can stop escalation without inventing a winner;
- no scenario requires the designer's private explanation.

## 3. Calculable state closure

Every authoritative register must answer:

```text
owner
range and initial value
transition trigger
effect on legal futures
recovery or reset
witness and capsule field
```

Priority closures:

- combat cycle reset;
- Integrity, injury, Prone, and repair;
- Reserve and care;
- party Cohesion, Reserve, Integrity, and Memory;
- beast and scenario clocks;
- magic and organ settlement;
- land access and active slots;
- Growth Marks and Rings;
- State Capsule conflicts and branch restore.

## 4. Physical play

Battle grids and figures are the primary spatial surface for encounters. Cards,
bottle caps, coins, stones, beads, ledgers, and forms remain optional state and
witness materials.

Acceptance:

- a group can run a combat with figures and a grid;
- a noncombat minigame can use a grid or diagrammatic spatial surface;
- every physical object has a visible state role;
- the table is not forced to use cards when ordinary objects work better.

## 5. Death, recovery, and T-Stop

Make the new terminal constitution playable rather than merely specified:

- zero Integrity does not automatically kill;
- no generic death-saving throw exists;
- Prone and named injuries are clear physical states;
- death is explicitly established and witnessed;
- death preserves traces, relationships, custody, and consequences;
- recovery has a declared care path;
- a player can stop without shame;
- a GM can preserve danger without coercive escalation.

## 6. State Capsules

Make stopping and returning usable at the table:

- character capsule;
- party capsule;
- scene and cell state;
- terminal status;
- pending event;
- branch parent;
- entropy record;
- recovery and resume condition.

Acceptance:

- a group can stop at Rest;
- restore the capsule later;
- identify the last shared witness;
- reject a stale or incompatible capsule;
- preserve a terminal body without erasing its history;
- branch without silently merging incompatible futures.

## 7. First ecology

Make the first creature and environmental bodies distinct in play. Each should
have needs, senses, resources, boundaries, legal responses, clocks, T-Stops,
traces, and Rest.

The ecology must support peaceful, strange, dangerous, and unresolved outcomes.
No bestiary entry should reduce to attack, damage, and death.

## 8. Magic and organs

Make one spell and two organs fully playable in host and cardless modes.

Every organ must show:

```text
input
output
cost
latency
scope
error values
T-Stop set
Rest condition
witness
```

## 9. Growth and capability

Growth must change what a character can legally do without becoming an
optimization treadmill.

- Growth Mark and Ring conversion is clear;
- active slots and Load are visible;
- current and maximum Integrity/Reserve are distinct;
- organs can be accepted, refused, repaired, or retired;
- specialization and breadth remain different power shapes.

Beta should also make organismic membrane growth playable. The current
`primary membrane -> secondary membrane` language is a useful scaffold for
discussion, but it must not become a class-stacking requirement. Characters
should be able to acquire individual abilities, organs, practices, senses,
boundaries, and Rest methods from any species through play.

```text
seed body
-> encounter, practice, relationship, or pressure
-> acquired species verb
-> cost, boundary, and receiving state
-> witnessed integration
-> changed organism and Rest practice
```

Acceptance:

- the four alpha species can appear throughout the population;
- Resident Profiles can be local specialists without being lesser beings;
- a character can acquire an individual ability from any species without taking
  a second class;
- every acquired ability declares its source, input, output, cost, boundary,
  refusal, and Rest condition;
- temporary, licensed, learned, borrowed, and integrated abilities are visibly
  distinct;
- breadth costs Load, maintenance, switching, latency, Reserve, or relationship
  work where the body requires it;
- the character record and State Capsule preserve the witnessed history of each
  acquisition;
- two characters with the same starting species can become mechanically and
  fictionally different through play;
- no character needs to reproduce an existing class permutation to be complete;
- player-facing agency is distinguished from capability or personhood.

## 10. Multiscale world systems

Make the organism chain playable across individual, party, household, community,
institution, settlement or dungeon, region, and world scales. Community, economy,
ecology, geology, weather, travel, care, conflict, knowledge, religion, magic,
and technology should interact through declared state and witnessed transitions,
not independent bookkeeping layers.

Acceptance:

- a scene identifies one primary dynamic and no more than a small number of
  supporting dynamics;
- bodies can move between live, active, latent, and dormant attention modes;
- zooming into a household, community, faction, or ecology inherits current
  aggregate constraints without contradiction;
- zooming out preserves changed stocks, relationships, obligations, witnesses,
  and unresolved consequences;
- a community, economy, ecology, or geological body has legal responses and a
  recovery or Rest condition rather than only descriptive lore;
- consequences can cross at least two organism levels without requiring the table
  to calculate unrelated populations or markets;
- the first Forgotten Realms examples work without a new world map;
- players encounter consequences as choices, requests, costs, relationships, and
  changed places rather than as administrative updates;
- the GM can prepare a responsive scene with a bounded calculation budget;
- a State Capsule preserves every cross-scale fact that changes the next legal
  future.

## 11. Unified physics and delight

Make the working unified physics available at every organism level without
requiring simultaneous simulation. Gravity, Dark Matter, Antimatter,
Bidirectional Recursion, Entropy, Phase, Projection, First Touch, Conservation,
T-Stop, Witness, and Care should be felt through play and exposed only when they
change meaningful legal futures.

Acceptance:

- Gravity appears as attractor basins, changing opportunity, habit, route,
  relationship, or Rest, not as a hidden universal modifier;
- Dark Matter has a source and observable effects and can become active through
  lawful touch or signal;
- Antimatter or phase opposition produces an explicit conversion, separation,
  interruption, residue, annihilation, or new phase;
- every persistent transition has a forward action and a reverse-readable
  witness path;
- Entropy is experienced as meaningful residue, not unexplained bookkeeping;
- Conservation and provenance prevent unearned resources, capabilities,
  permissions, knowledge, and return paths;
- Phase and synchronization make timing and coordination playable;
- Projection protects private and hidden state without making the world arbitrary;
- First Touch activates NPCs and systems only when a relevant interaction occurs;
- Care creates or repairs attractors through declared inputs and costs;
- ordinary play, bounded play, and persistent play are distinct table options;
- a GM can stop tracking a detail when it no longer changes a legal future;
- beta playtests record delight, surprise, burden, and whether the world felt
  responsive and memorable.
- Tier 1 physics, Tier 2 player systems, Tier 3 world procedures, Tier 4
  lightweight systems, and Tier 5 optional modules remain visibly distinct;
- no optional module is required to understand or enjoy the golden seed;
- each active system has a physical or written representation small enough to
  use at the table.
- the Familiar System adds identity, refusal, privacy, Rest, and departure rather
  than free action economy;
- a player choosing no familiar retains a complete character and campaign path;

## 12. Knowledge, language, and culture

Make knowledge a situated flow through perception, interpretation, language,
memory, relationship, and community rather than a universal lore database.

Acceptance:

- knowledge records source, scope, custody, visibility, state, and witness when
  those fields change legal futures;
- players can distinguish sensed, observed, inferred, taught, shared, trusted,
  contested, private, and forgotten knowledge;
- language changes access, authority, intimacy, ritual, refusal, and relationship
  without reducing every exchange to a skill roll;
- partial comprehension and misunderstanding produce honest state rather than
  arbitrary nonsense;
- NPC jobs provide public functional knowledge while relationships reveal deeper
  individual knowledge;
- community knowledge can sediment into custom, rumor, job training, ritual, or
  public memory;
- knowledge can remain private or contested without the GM treating hidden state
  as arbitrary;
- the first Forgotten Realms examples work with existing setting languages and
  registers;
- knowledge provenance survives a State Capsule return;
- a table can choose ordinary, bounded, or persistent depth for an exchange.

## 13. Campaign module

Produce at least one complete, runnable campaign module that exercises the shared
constitution across a populated world rather than presenting disconnected
scenarios.

Acceptance:

- the module has a player-facing premise and entry point;
- the GM can run it without private designer explanation;
- named characters have jobs, needs, boundaries, and lawful responses;
- the module contains communities, factions, resources, ecology, monsters,
  knowledge boundaries, and spatial surfaces;
- growth, jobs, worship or divine pressure, treasure, and relationships can
  develop through play;
- multiple lawful paths and endings exist;
- the module supplies State Capsule stops, return points, and persistent
  consequences;
- the module can be used with the Player Guide, GM Guide, and Bestiary projections.
- the module may be an original campaign or a documented conversion of an
  existing campaign host;
- conversion preserves host content while adding jobs, body projections,
  relationships, ecology, T-Stops, witnesses, and State Capsules.

## 14. Accessibility and care

Beta work must test:

- controller-first or accessible table operation;
- readable forms;
- clear visual state;
- low-bookkeeping play paths;
- optional complexity;
- safe stopping and recovery;
- no required hidden knowledge for basic participation.

## 15. Documentation and source truth

Beta goals must not create a second contradictory rulebook. Every promoted rule
has one source of truth, one glossary meaning, one worked example, and one
acceptance observation.
