# Alpha 4 Audit and Procedure Infusion

*Beta garden bridge: preserve the Alpha 4 constitution while turning its most
important table questions into runnable procedures.*

## Provenance

Gemini's `research/DEEP_HEARTH_FULL_ANALYSIS_FOR_MEL.md` is a trusted repository-
level audit of Deep Hearth 0.1.0-alpha.4. It confirms that the written Alpha 4
body has a coherent engine: four capacities, Demand bands, the six-cycle clock,
T-Stops, first-touch population, State Capsules, the paper State Deck, and the
Responsive Neighborhood architecture.

This document does not replace `source/`. It records the Beta work that follows
from the audit. The distinction is deliberate:

```text
Alpha source       -> authoritative rule and vocabulary
Gemini audit       -> validated architectural reading and seam discovery
Beta procedure     -> proposed runnable clarification
human play report  -> evidence for promotion and revision
```

The audit is validated against the repository. Human play is still required to
validate whether a procedure is intuitive, enjoyable, and affordable at the
table.

## Procedure Test

Every Beta procedure must answer:

```text
trigger
state owner
player or GM input
cost and latency
uncertainty source
resolution steps
changed state
legal T-Stops
witness
recovery or return
```

If one field is not relevant, the procedure says why. If ownership or the
receiving state is unknown, the result remains `UNKNOWN`, `PENDING`, or fiction;
the table does not invent arithmetic.

These procedures are candidates until real play promotes, revises, or retires
them. The audit's broader findings remain useful even when a particular table
procedure changes.

## Hydration Rule

Infuse the existing garden rather than creating a second rulebook:

1. Put player-facing steps in the player-facing or session document.
2. Put hidden-state and preparation steps in the GM-facing document.
3. Put physical representations in the cards/cardless and toolkit documents.
4. Put acceptance observations in the playtest and graduation documents.
5. Link to the Alpha source instead of restating a competing core rule.

The desired result is not maximum coverage. It is fewer moments where a player or
facilitator must ask, “What do we do now?”

## Open Evidence

The following remain especially important to test:

- the Alpha 4 Demand, capacity, help, and pressure choices;
- ownership of party resources versus scene resources;
- the effect of a strained neighborhood transition;
- the cost and usefulness of land retuning;
- whether away-ticks create wonder or bookkeeping;
- whether the procedure cards preserve refusal, privacy, and Rest;
- whether the table can save and return without reconstructing missing state.
