# Beta Playtest Protocol

This is deliberately light real-play evidence, not a laboratory burden.

## Before play

Record:

```text
group size
participant roles and whether they authored the rules
rules version
materials used
facilitator
scenario
starting capsule
questions being tested
```

Do not explain missing rules privately during play without recording the ruling.
If the group invents a ruling, mark whether it was a repair, a local card, or a
core-rule gap.

## During play

Observe:

- where players ask what they can do;
- where a cost is unclear;
- whether a refusal feels legal and safe;
- whether the grid and figures make state visible;
- whether computation creates a meaningful choice;
- whether the GM can offer danger without forcing one outcome;
- whether Rest feels like a valid result;
- whether death or incapacitation is understood;
- which rules are joyful, slow, confusing, or invisible.
- whether Gravity felt like changing possibility rather than a hidden bonus;
- whether Dark Matter was intriguing and traceable rather than arbitrary;
- whether phase opposition produced understandable choices rather than bookkeeping;
- whether a forward action had a clear witness and return path;
- whether Care created a satisfying new possibility;
- whether players could distinguish what a body sensed from what it inferred;
- whether partial comprehension felt meaningful rather than punitive;
- whether knowledge provenance and private boundaries remained legible;
- whether cultural details emerged through play instead of becoming lore lecture;
- which system tier was active and whether its depth was appropriate;
- whether an optional system felt genuinely optional;
- whether a rule created an adventure, a meaningful choice, or only maintenance;
- whether the campaign module could be run without designer intervention;
- whether the module offered enough content and structure for another session;

For the Alpha 4 procedure infusion, deliberately observe these seams:

- on an opposed tie, whether both bodies understand the resulting strained
  contact and their legal next choices;
- during a multi-cycle action, whether the interrupt example makes reaction timing
  unambiguous;
- whether players can tell ordinary Rest from material somatic care;
- whether attention has a concrete cost in the current habitat;
- whether land retuning feels like a meaningful choice rather than free setup;
- whether a beast away-tick creates a memorable return state without chore work;
- whether a generic resident becomes deeper at a clear first-touch boundary;
- whether asking, investigating, and exposing knowledge preserve scope and privacy;
- whether every neighborhood register has an obvious owner and payer;
- whether a strained or failed neighborhood action produces a lawful changed future.

## After play

Record:

```text
what surprised the group
what changed state
what was hard to calculate
what was never worth calculating
which terminal states appeared
what the group wanted to know next
what they would play again
```

A playtest is successful when it produces trustworthy learning, not when every
rule is praised or every scene reaches a planned ending.

Record bookkeeping burden separately from rules confusion. A system may be
understood and still be too expensive to keep active. Note which registers were
never consulted, which were delightful to touch, and which consequences players
remembered without prompting.

The facilitator signs or otherwise attests that the report describes an actual
human table session. Software simulation, automated transcripts, and solo
walkthroughs may supplement the report but do not count as qualifying beta
promotion evidence.
