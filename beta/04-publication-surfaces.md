# Publication Surfaces

The technical alpha, beta game book, and mature book family are different views
over one constitution.

## Player Handbook

Players need:

- character creation;
- capacities, resources, conditions, and actions;
- movement and grid play;
- relationships and refusal;
- equipment, magic, and growth;
- what they can perceive;
- how to Rest, stop, and return.
- how ordinary, bounded, and persistent play differ;
- how jobs, relationships, and capabilities become deeper through witnessed play;
- how hidden state can exert influence without becoming arbitrary;
- how knowledge, language, partial comprehension, and private memory affect legal
  choices;
- how the Familiar System changes play from direct operation to relational
  operation, while keeping no-familiar play complete;

Players do not need hidden GM state, encounter construction algorithms, or the
full implementation vocabulary in order to make meaningful choices.

## GM's Guide

The GM needs:

- encounter programs;
- ecology and creature construction;
- clocks and resource circuits;
- hidden state and visibility;
- faction, community, and world-cell design;
- T-Stop and death handling;
- State Capsules, branches, and recovery;
- procedures for lawful improvisation.
- unified physics invariants and the attention budget;
- zooming, aggregation, and cross-scale consequence propagation;
- NPC depth and persistent-character escalation;
- Antimatter, phase opposition, and explicit incompatibility handling.
- knowledge provenance, signal, translation, and cultural emergence.

The system tier and build-order document determines which procedures belong in
the first beta book and which remain GM-facing or optional modules.

The GM-facing publication must also include at least one complete campaign
module, with a player-facing entry, GM-facing hidden state, named characters,
jobs, factions, ecology, monsters, scenarios, growth, treasure, and return points.

The module may be original or a conversion layer over an existing campaign host.
Conversion procedures belong in the GM's Guide; protected host text and setting
content remain outside the open Basin rules body.

## Monster Manual / Bestiary

The Bestiary contains complete GM-facing creature bodies:

```text
habitat
needs
senses
resources
boundaries
legal responses
registers and clocks
T-Stop set
traces
Rest
```

Players may encounter creatures through perception and consequence without
receiving the complete entry. Surprise is a visibility boundary, not a hidden
rule that contradicts the creature's actual body.

## Shared source of truth

Every public surface must agree about:

```text
state
cost
agency
T-Stop
witness
Rest
recovery
gravity and attractors
projection and privacy
witness and reverse-readable history
phase and incompatibility
knowledge source and scope
language and interpretation
```

The Player Handbook hides implementation detail. The GM's Guide exposes enough
to tend the world. The Bestiary preserves secrets without making the creature
arbitrary.
